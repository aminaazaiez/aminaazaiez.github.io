I am a postdoctoral researcher at the CNRS, based at [Centre d'analyse et de mathématique sociales (CAMS)](https://cams.ehess.fr/), where I work with [Jean-Pierre Nadal](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=fr&user=Z1eDue8AAAAJ&view_op=list_works) as part of the [ReaCh project](https://anr.fr/Projet-ANR-23-CE40-0023) "Reaction-Diffusion: New Challenges".

My research falls broadly within the field of complex systems and their applications to the social sciences. I currently study the spatial diffusion of social movements combining mathematical modeling and data analysis.


I hold a PhD in applied mathematics from Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne, supervised by [Antoine Mandel](https://sites.google.com/site/antoinedavidmandel/), and MSc degrees in fundamental physics and physics of complex systems from Université Paris-Saclay. My PhD thesis studied group interactions in politics and inequalities in access to political institutions, using hypergraph theory.
